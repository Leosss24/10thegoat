# 10theGOAT — Electric Blue brand system

## Brand idea

10theGOAT is a football-knowledge arena: competitive, precise and digital. The goat mark is the distinctive symbol; the shield is the institutional logo; the horizontal wordmark is the hero and social lock-up. Real players, club badges and third-party marks are not part of the core brand system.

## Logo family

- `10thegoat-shield-raster.png`: primary/institutional logo. This detailed raster master is the authoritative shield; use it without flattening or vectorising its rendered finish.
- `10thegoat-wordmark.svg`: secondary horizontal lock-up with “TEST YOUR KNOWLEDGE”. Use on heroes, banners and wide canvases.
- `10thegoat-mark.svg`: goat symbol. Use for compact navigation, avatars and decorative brand moments.
- `favicon.svg`: simplified small-size mark. Do not use as a large logo.

Keep clear space equal to one eye-to-eye width of the goat around every logo. Do not rotate, recolor, stretch, add club marks or place the logos on busy imagery without a dark overlay.

## Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#080B10` | Page canvas |
| Surface | `#121820` | Cards and controls |
| Raised surface | `#1A2330` | Secondary surfaces |
| Electric Blue | `#32A7FF` | Primary actions and brand signal |
| Light Blue | `#74D5FF` | Focus, glow and highlights |
| Cool White | `#F5F8FC` | Primary text |
| Blue Grey | `#87919E` | Secondary text |

Electric Blue is a signal, not a fill for large reading surfaces. Cool White on Background and Background on Electric Blue are the preferred high-contrast pairs.

## Typography

The UI uses the native `Inter`/system sans stack for speed and multilingual coverage. Display headings and the wordmark use a condensed, italic sports treatment with `Arial Narrow` fallback. Headings may be uppercase; paragraphs and controls remain sentence case for legibility.

## Spacing and shape

Spacing follows an 8 px base rhythm: 8, 16, 24 and 32 px. Corner radii are 10 px for controls, 16 px for cards and 24 px for large game panels. Blue glows are restrained and always paired with a solid boundary.

## Imagery and motion

Use abstract arena architecture, pitch geometry, diagonal energy and particles. Avoid real players, copyrighted kits, club badges and photorealistic likenesses in owned brand artwork. Motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; focus states must remain visible in Light Blue.
